2. Each provider attempt has a hard 180-second limit without counting retries.
   Any explicitly eligible retry receives its own separate 180-second attempt.
   Automatic redispatch remains disabled.

3. When a Python, prompt, or integration error occurs:
   - stop fail-closed;
   - preserve the exact failing session;
   - identify the exact owner;
   - make the smallest general fix;
   - run a focused test for the causative seam;
   - replay that exact saved session;
   - after it succeeds, start a completely fresh run from fixture 1;
   - use a different story direction on each fresh run.

4. Optimize time. Do not run the full 2,000+ test suite after every narrow fix.
   Use focused tests and exact replay. Use a broad gate only for a stable final
   candidate when it materially adds confidence.

5. Routine in-scope fixes, focused tests, commits, pushes, scenario rotations,
   and fresh attempts do not require repeated permission requests.

6. Keep user-facing updates short and list-based.

7. Avoid subagents unless genuinely necessary or explicitly requested.

8. Fresh runs must vary story capabilities and prompt direction. Do not reduce
   qualification to repeated closed-door or verification/binary-question scenes.

9. A front door is not required to remain closed unless that individual story
   establishes it as a constraint.

10. Minor DeepSeek deviations should normally become provisional world facts.
    Do not make a second Writer call for harmless staging, pacing, localized
    omissions, or small additions. A second Writer call is for a severe failure.

11. Ordinary Ted hard protections:
    - do not invent Ted's dialogue;
    - do not invent Ted's private thoughts, feelings, memories, or state.

12. Adult-route Ted freedom is broader inside an already authorized adult
    interaction. Plausible immediate physical action and limited in-scene

    withdrawal, lasting decisions, memories, or permanent preferences.

    Small additions around an existing event may be provisional.

    uncertain, but the event itself must continue to exist. New harmless details
    around it are allowed provisionally.

15. If the same DeepSeek hard failure class appears twice:
    - stop local prompt tweaking and root rotation;
    - preserve the exact packet;
    - compare exact same-packet samples with varied-packet samples;
    - use the same validators;
    - do not mutate canon;
    - ask the existing ChatGPT Pro CERA review chat once;
    - keep DeepSeek as Writer/Adult model.

16. ChatGPT Pro and Claude are advisory reviewers. Codex remains responsible
    for code, evidence, and technical decisions.
