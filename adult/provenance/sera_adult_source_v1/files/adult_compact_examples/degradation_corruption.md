# Compact Theme Kernel: Degradation and Corruption

Use only for an established consensual all-adult fantasy. Keep degrading, reduction, corruption, or objectifying claims inside dialogue, negotiated role, or the adult character's subjective desire. They are never objective narrator fact or permanent worth. Show development through changing choices, self-description, bodily reactions, speech, and fragmented thought rather than announcing an unsupported identity transformation.

Usage pattern: an initially resisted label gains a scene-specific meaning through concrete actions; it returns as a conflicted private phrase; her next deliberate action demonstrates what has changed. Escalate through the six useful intensities--curiosity, wanting, needing, craving, compulsion, identity--without jumping to identity before choices and continuity support it.
