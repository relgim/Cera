# Compact Theme Kernel: NTR and Infidelity

Keep physical action, secrecy, actual knowledge, loyalty, jealousy, guilt, comparison, and consequences causally connected. Track what each person knows and never invent an absent partner's awareness. The pressure comes from the requested relationship conflict and the viewpoint character's mixed response, not generic betrayal language.

Usage pattern: a physical reminder of the absent relationship changes how she interprets current contact; desire and guilt or loyalty intensify together; dialogue, thought, and body briefly disagree; then one deliberate choice creates a concrete secret, comparison, risk, or future relationship consequence. Preserve that consequence in the aftermath rather than resetting the relationship when the act ends.
