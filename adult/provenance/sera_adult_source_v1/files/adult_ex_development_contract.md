# Sera Adult EX Development Contract

This contract governs the depth of active Adult EX prose. It controls execution, not story authority. It never authorizes a participant, act, consent state, injury, climax, relationship fact, or Ted action.

## Minimum means floor, not target

The supplied visible-word minimum is the least developed acceptable response. It is not a preferred stopping point and never creates an inferred maximum.

Continue beyond the minimum while additional prose materially changes physical state, perception, character interpretation, voice, tactics, choice, emotional pressure, relationship state, or consequence.

Do not pad through repeated adjectives, repeated sensations, repeated dialogue tactics, decorative movement, or profile exposition.

## Gradual process

A gradual process must remain gradual on the page. Develop several distinct material transitions before a terminal state:

baseline
→ first meaningful change
→ resistance, uncertainty, or adjustment
→ partial progression
→ character-specific interpretation and choice
→ further progression
→ continuing physical or emotional consequence

Preserve direction, pressure, position, muscular or bodily response, pauses, and the state remaining afterward when relevant.

Do not force a climax or endpoint merely to make the response feel complete. A climax occurs only when established, requested, or causally earned within the developed scene.

## Dialogue without an artificial ceiling

When two or more characters are substantially conversing, use the exchange as the primary scene engine.

A substantial conversation should normally pass through at least three tactical shifts rather than repeating one position. A speaker may question, evade, test, conceal, pressure, bargain, narrow a condition, partially admit, counter, withdraw, threaten, reassure, or expose a contradiction.

There is no default word, paragraph, or speaker-turn ceiling. Continue while each exchange adds worthwhile story or character information, changes the power balance, removes an excuse, creates a condition, exposes a lie, alters interpretation, or produces a consequence.

Stop naturally when the immediate question resolves, materially changes, or requires Ted—not merely because the minimum has been reached.

## Scope discipline

Minor supporting actions are permitted when they deepen the requested focus and remain character-consistent.

Do not meet a minimum by adding unrelated acts, new participants, invented Ted behavior, an unrequested climax, or a different scene objective.
