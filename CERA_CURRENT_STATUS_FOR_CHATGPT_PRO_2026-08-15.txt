6. CURRENT UNCOMMITTED FIX

The production change is deliberately limited to the adult runtime's context
and candidate-path construction.

A. Safe accepted-record projection

- Removes complete acceptance/audit receipt bodies from the Writer-visible safe
  projection.
- Keeps story-relevant ordinary_record, adult_projection, and provisional_canon
  fields.
- Keeps route, accepted turn identity, recording status, and a SHA-256 binding
  to the original full receipt.
- If the projection is still over 40,000 characters, it drops only the oldest
  completed records until the bound is met.
- It does not drop unfinished records.
- It records how many older completed records were omitted.

B. Genesis projection compaction

- Detects typed genesis_record_projections instead of flattening their entire
  payload_json trees.
- Emits one concise fact per Genesis record.
- Retains identity, claim, authority, certainty, truth status, record type,
  visibility, subject, and exact source-content hash binding.
- Does not expose the huge expanded payload_json to the Adult Scene Writer.

C. Visibility normalization

- owner_private, private, system_private, character_private, creator_private,
  creator-only, and adult_role_private map to adult-role-private context.
- public, shared, and branch_internal_unspecified map to safe/public context.
- shared context may retain a subject/knowledge owner without becoming private.
- Explicit public context still rejects an unexpected private owner.

D. Protected prose leak guard

- The leak guard now checks the raw safe records as well as the final safe
  projection, facts, and story boundaries.

E. Windows path budget

- The full 256-bit scope hash remains in the typed candidate scope and custody.
- The physical candidate workspace uses a 96-bit scope prefix under a short
  `protected_adult\c\...` path.
- This mirrors the already-bounded regeneration workspace and leaves enough
  room for Writer-view staging components on Windows.

No Planner logic, DeepSeek prompt, adult validator, consent rule, Ted rule,
retry policy, acceptance policy, or provider ceiling is changed by this fix.
