7. ROOT AZ DEFECT: EXACT CAUSE

The final qualification validator examines the Sol provider ledger and permits
failure events only when they belong to a validated explicit retry chain.

The defect was a condition in:
src/cera/pi_scene/qualification.py::_validate_provider_delta

Before the correction, the code only permitted retried failures whose terminal
state was pretransport_failed or provider_failed. It still rejected a retried
provider_completed_post_validation_failed event even when:
- the event belonged to the exact Planner stage;
- the retry chain was validated;
- both retries were explicitly authorized;
- the third attempt succeeded; and
- final operation counts matched exactly.

Root AZ's first two Planner attempts used the valid terminal state
provider_completed_post_validation_failed because transport completed but the
typed result was invalid. Provider-stage retry correctly treated those events
as retryable. Final qualification accounting contradicted that earlier valid
classification and called them unrelated failures.

Provider-free reconstruction before the fix reproduced:
StateConflictError: qualification Sol ledger contains an unrelated failure

This is a deterministic Python accounting bug. It is not evidence that the
story, DeepSeek Writer, Recorder, accepted branch, or recovered Planner result
was bad.

12. CURRENT HARD AND PROVISIONAL BOUNDARY

Ordinary hard failures include:
- Invented Ted dialogue.
- Invented Ted private state, thoughts, feelings, or memories.
- Invented or overridden consent/withdrawal.
- Major lasting decisions attributed to Ted without authority.
- Severe immutable identity, age, or family-relation corruption.
- Erasing the existence of an accepted major event.
- Unusable, incoherent, empty, or completely off-task output.
- Python custody, privacy, identity, deterministic, or transaction failure.

Normally provisional rather than fatal:
- Minor staging differences.
- Harmless new event details.
- Small action ordering differences.
- Local object or communication details.
- Brief aftermath.
- Compatible capability details.
- Ordinary local continuity imperfections that do not cross a hard boundary.

Hard always wins when hard and provisional conditions coexist.
