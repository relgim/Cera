0071: 
0072: When the creator authorizes a Codex-to-ChatGPT-Pro progression tranche, follow
0073: `docs/authority/CODEX_PROGRESS_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md` in addition to the ordinary
0074: roadmap gate.
0075: 
0076: - Treat three substantial progressions as the maximum, never a quota.
0077: - Stop early when a terminal blocker makes the remaining progression unsafe or
0078:   irrelevant; preserve the useful diagnostic evidence in the checkpoint.
0079: - Establish the required lossless backup and safe local Git baseline before the
0080:   first governed tranche changes the repository.
0081: - Complete a local checkpoint commit after the bounded tranche and verify the
0082:   actual diff, active source, tests, and effects before claiming completion.
0083: - Write the reusable Pro review request under `.chatgpt/pro-review/checkpoints/`
0084:   and send the request path plus checkpoint SHA.
0085: - Stop completely after submitting the request. Silence, an acceptance token,
0086:   or an advisory review is not creator authorization for another tranche.
0087: - ChatGPT Pro reviews the real checkpoint and may recommend the next two or
0088:   three progressions; the creator alone authorizes their implementation.

0094: ## Task selection and precedence
0095: 
0096: The first tranche is defined by the initial stabilization prompt. Every later
0097: tranche is selected by ChatGPT Pro after reviewing the preceding checkpoint.
0098: 
0099: Codex must not self-authorize additional architecture, prompt systems, model
0100: ladders, Adult activation, production binding, deployment, or unrelated cleanup
0101: merely because the code makes it convenient.
0102: 
0103: Priority order:
0104: 
0105: 1. **P0 — truth and change control:** Git, active identity consistency,
0106:    authority/privacy defects, incorrect current documentation, and
0107:    transactional/restart defects.
0108: 2. **P1 — exact current-route reliability:** current-version end-to-end
0109:    qualification, failure recovery, state/prose agreement, and deterministic
0110:    continuity checks.
0111: 3. **P2 — creator product evidence:** varied creator-labeled turns, voice
0112:    distinction, realism, first-candidate acceptance, factual invention, and
0113:    scene development.
0114: 4. **P3 — latency and maintainability:** measured routing changes, verifier
0115:    value, module decomposition, CI, and tooling.
0116: 5. **P4 — Adult live publication, production binding, promotion, and
0117:    deployment:** only after separate creator authority and prerequisite evidence.
0118: 
0119: A lower-priority task must not displace an unresolved higher-priority defect
0120: without an explicit creator or ChatGPT Pro decision.
