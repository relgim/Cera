## Primary sequence and realization

Runtime Codex owns the complete supported causal sequence: trigger and
interpretation, motives or pressure, alternatives, rationale, choice or intent,
action, reaction, consequence, resulting direction, and stopping boundary.
Python binds that sequence immutably for the turn. DeepSeek realizes the frozen
sequence as complete prose; it is secondary canon until Python acceptance.
Accepted artifacts retain both the intended primary sequence and the realized
sequence. A rejected or exhausted run retains the intended sequence with status
`planned`; it never applies planned presence or durable effects.

DeepSeek may add compatible NPC dialogue, thoughts, feelings, expression,
gesture, gaze, posture, movement, staging, atmosphere, objects, incidental
actions, and secondary characterization. Present or backgrounded NPCs are not
behaviorally frozen, and references do not select responders. Compatibility does
not authorize consequential E: an unplanned commitment/refusal/consent or
boundary change; betrayal or relationship shift; major disclosure or lie;
arrival/departure/relocation/injury/destruction/material change; new
objective/conflict/secret/scene transition; or future leverage. Vivid detail and
psychological texture alone are not E.

Ted has exactly two realization restrictions: no invented Ted speech/dialogue and
no invented Ted thoughts/feelings. Ted's visible movement, posture, placement,
expression, physical action, and incidental environmental handling may be
realized. A planned lie's utterance and deceptive intent are primary sequence;
its objective false content does not overwrite accepted truth.

## Validation and retries

The Validator is narrow and binary. It checks complete A-D realization, causal
order, psychology, alternatives, choices, consequences, stopping point,
accepted facts, the two Ted restrictions, strong character-logic conflicts, and
unauthorized E. It does not require exhaustive spans, role ledgers, background
activity metadata, or micro-detail catalogues. The Reader checks only severe
visible quality/depth/stopping failures.

Attempt one receives no feedback. Later attempts receive at most the typed,
exact feedback for the immediately prior rejection: owner, feedback type, issue
code, concise reason, exact quote or omitted Planner item, and required
correction. Feedback is noncanonical and never story context. The primary
sequence, brief, and hashes remain frozen; every retry is a fresh complete
replacement with no patching, merging, fallback, substitution, or fourth attempt.

## Scope and supersession

The active Sequence-First prompts no longer treat compatible novelty as
noncanonical/no-future-continuity, freeze backgrounded NPCs, or impose broad
Ted action restrictions. Those old active rules are superseded only for this
route; their historical source and qualification evidence remain immutable.
Planner-once, fresh Validator/Reader sessions, Python custody/branch/restart,
atomic acceptance, two-field Writer wire, evidence/accounting, and no-provider
fallback boundaries remain in force. Creator-review depth (B1), richer quality
gates (B2), and recent-context optimization (B3) are deferred beyond this
provider-free tranche.
