# Pending Topics After the Modular-Prompt Phase

**Status:** advisory backlog; not implementation authority  
**Recorded:** 2026-07-30  
**Owner:** Codex technical planning, pending creator review and a later ChatGPT Pro phase

This document preserves topics discussed during creator planning so they are
not lost while the modular DeepSeek prompt phase is handled. Nothing here
authorizes implementation, provider calls, live Adult publication, Genesis
mutation, route promotion, or production work.

## 1. New facts in generated prose

Define separate handling for:

- reversible presentation texture;
- provisional biography, habit, routine, or history claims;
- creator-approved canonical additions.

Accepted visible prose must not silently promote every generated claim into
Genesis, objective events, character state, or durable memory. The later phase
must decide how provisional claims are identified, displayed for review,
retrieved without being mistaken for authority, approved, corrected, expired,
or rejected.

## 2. Bounded protected-user realization

Consider replacing the current strict Ted-action boundary with an explicit,
bounded policy resembling the useful non-sentient player treatment in Sera.
Potentially permitted scope is limited to restating supplied actions,
maintaining established position/contact, and minimal non-branching connective
motion. It must not invent Ted's dialogue, thoughts, feelings, consent,
refusal, strategy, consequential choice, or a new scene-changing action.

The contract, verifier, review UI, and tests must agree on the exact boundary.

## 3. Adult-content clarification and update diagnosis

Create a readable report intended for a separately selected adult-content
authoring AI. It should explain what CERA lacks and ask for the exact missing
material rather than assigning the work to ChatGPT Pro.

For each gap, include:

- content family and Adult ON/EX mode;
- missing or weak vocabulary, craft guidance, micro-example, continuity rule,
  physiology explanation, sound/action binding, buildup, climax, or aftermath;
- the observed sanitized symptom and frequency;
- what existing CERA material already covers;
- the exact clarification, example, terminology, or rewrite requested;
- constraints preventing examples from adding canon, consent, participants,
  acts, or causal events;
- acceptance criteria and the later test needed to close the gap.

The report should be readable prose with a compact checklist, not an internal
database dump. It may be generated from structured gap records, but the target
AI must not need to understand CERA's implementation to answer it.

## 4. Creator-correction diagnosis

When the creator submits a correction through the future review control, retain
a structured, privacy-safe correction record and generate a readable diagnosis
covering:

- what was wrong or missing;
- the affected behavior class rather than only the example turn;
- likely owner: creator-source ambiguity, Genesis/retrieval, Codex Reasoner,
  DeepSeek Composer/prompt/craft, deterministic Python, Sol verifier/review, or
  SillyTavern/UI transport;
- evidence and confidence;
- proposed correction and regression test;
- whether the issue is isolated, recurring, or already covered by another gap.

The diagnosis must not automatically rewrite prompts, code, Genesis, Adult
craft, or durable story truth. Repeated findings should be deduplicated into a
shared root-cause correction package.

## 5. Post-display creator review

Preserve the planned asynchronous review behavior:

- DeepSeek candidate prose may be displayed after deterministic validation;
- Sol reviews in the background;
- every finding, including Critical, is shown with reasons;
- the review card is removed from visible chat after creator action;
- a compact audit receipt survives without cluttering later model context;
- unavailable required Codex/Sol stages produce an error with no fallback.

A later creator decision must distinguish overridable critical prose/continuity
findings from hard authority, privacy, branch, protected-user, and
consent/capacity invalidity.

## 6. Model route during implementation

DeepSeek V4 Flash is the current implementation and local SillyTavern default
under D-158. Pro remains explicit-only and historical Pro evidence is unchanged.
Flash quality, latency, structured-output reliability, and prompt behavior must
be qualified before any promotion claim.
