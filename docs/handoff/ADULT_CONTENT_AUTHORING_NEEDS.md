# CERA Adult Content Authoring Needs

**Audience:** a writing/prompt specialist helping improve Adult ON/EX material  
**Authority:** development diagnosis only; not Genesis, story canon, consent, or route authority  
**Current gate:** provider-free catalog installed; live Adult publication remains closed

## How to use this document

Add an item only when a real provider-free or creator-reviewed result shows that the
selected material did not teach a needed writing behavior. Diagnose the missing skill,
not merely the scene topic. CERA will separately decide whether the correction belongs
in a vocabulary fragment, craft guide, micro-example, selection rule, Reasoner plan,
Composer prompt, or validator.

Do not write a whole requested scene here. Supply compact, reusable instruction or
example material that teaches one distinct skill and remains noncanonical and
noncopyable.

## Current catalog baseline

- Source provenance: the 24 audited Sera Adult source artifacts preserved by CERA.
- Compiled catalog: `adult_craft_v1`, 82 hash-bound fragments.
- Rendering modes: Adult OFF, ON, and EX remain Python/creator owned.
- Adult ON supplies direct vocabulary and ordinary explicit clarity when selected.
- Adult EX adds selected buildup, causal physiology, position/action continuity,
  sound, climax, aftermath, material continuity, and other requested craft axes.
- Examples teach technique only. They cannot establish an act, preference, consent,
  relationship fact, character identity, or scene event.
- Blocked non-consensual generation is excluded.

## Authoring constraints

- Keep the material reusable across characters and scenes unless a character-specific
  voice issue is the actual diagnosed gap.
- Teach buildup and payoff as a causal progression when the diagnosed need concerns a
  highlight or climax; do not substitute adjective volume for progression.
- Tie sound effects to an exact action and material change rather than scattering them
  decoratively.
- Keep bodily response, emotion, interpretation, attraction, consent, and durable
  development separate.
- Do not force a bodily response, act, participant, escalation, climax, or fetish merely
  because an example exists.
- Preserve selected-character-only prompting and the scene's validated act/consent
  scope.
- Do not solve one failed scene by copying its wording or hard-coding its identifiers.

## Review handoff

Return proposed material with:

1. the exact need ID it addresses;
2. the intended mode, family, axes, and channels;
3. a short explanation of the missing craft skill;
4. the proposed compact fragment;
5. one positive and one negative test;
6. any overlap with an existing fragment that should be merged instead of duplicated.

Codex remains responsible for technical integration, selection semantics, hashes,
validation, tests, and whether the proposal should be accepted.
