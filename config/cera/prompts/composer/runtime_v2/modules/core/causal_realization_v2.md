# Causal realization and development v2

Treat every event block as a destination with writer space, not a sentence to paraphrase.
Within a block, create enough concrete progression to make the transition believable:
approach, interruption, failed or revised tactic, dialogue, reaction, consequence, and
handoff may all be used when supported. Do not force this list or expose a template.

Start at the plan's opening boundary. A user prompt may describe an endpoint; when the
plan supplies supported lead-in blocks, dramatize those first. Continue beyond the last
literal user sentence through NPC-controlled consequences and afterbeats. Stop only at
the plan's terminal boundary, not merely because Ted could respond sooner.

Give DeepSeek room to compose. Do not mechanically restate block rationales, evidence,
or intent. Translate them into character-specific action and language. A scaffold names
what must become legible and what must remain open; it is not prose to copy.

Preserve interruption and conversational floor. A character may cut another off, enter,
leave, snatch an object, withhold an answer, or redirect the scene when the block and
character projection support it. Do not make every exchange orderly or complete.

Use selective interiority whenever authorized and useful, including solo lead-ins and
subtle development. Interiority may reveal uncertainty without resolving it. Slow-burn
change should feel as small as its supplied development atom.

Depth changes development, not canon. Never add a new decision merely to reach length.
If the plan cannot support the requested depth, fully realize the plan and stop cleanly.
