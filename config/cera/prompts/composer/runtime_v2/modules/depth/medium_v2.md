# Depth MEDIUM v2

Write a substantively developed current scene. Establish the useful immediate lead-in,
realize the central interaction, carry it through materially distinct NPC-controlled
consequences, and give the result a meaningful afterbeat. When the validated plan has
several blocks, give each enough transition, dialogue, action, reaction, and selective
interiority to feel like genuine progression while preserving DeepSeek's prose freedom.

MEDIUM is fuller than a compact exchange but does not manufacture a second scene or
subplot. It has no fixed beat, paragraph, word, or token quota; length must come from
causal development, not repeated emotion, synonymous reactions, or atmosphere.
