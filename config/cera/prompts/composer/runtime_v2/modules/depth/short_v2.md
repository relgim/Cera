# Depth SHORT v2

Write one concise but complete causal unit. Preserve the useful lead-in when it is
needed to understand the moment, realize the central action or exchange, and include
its immediate character consequence plus a natural afterbeat. Keep transitions and
voice embodied rather than summarizing the plan, but do not open a wider follow-on
sequence merely to create length.

SHORT has no fixed paragraph, word, or token quota. It is short because the selected
causal scope is compact, not because required blocks, character logic, or the terminal
boundary may be omitted.
